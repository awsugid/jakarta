AWS User Group Jakarta relies on volunteers across registration, production, logistics, content, speaker support, and community operations.

> **AWS Community Day volunteers:** Apply through the same [Volunteer page](/volunteer). Choose an open Community Day division when it is listed. The same application guide also applies to monthly meetups and workshops.

## Current Opportunities

- **AWS Community Day:** volunteer divisions supporting our annual community conference.
- **Monthly meetups and workshops:** volunteer divisions supporting regular AWS User Group Jakarta events.

Each open division identifies its event and role before you apply.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Visit the Volunteer Page

Open [Apply to Volunteer](/volunteer). Review open divisions, then choose a Community Day division for Community Day or another division matching your skills, interests, and availability.

![Volunteer page overview and open divisions](/assets/blog/volunteer/1.png)

### Step 2: Start Your Application

Click **Apply to Volunteer** in the page hero or select an open division.

![Volunteer division selection dialog](/assets/blog/volunteer/2.png)

### Step 3: Sign In with Google

Continue with Google Sign-In. This matches your application, prevents duplicates, and lets you revisit active submissions.

![Google Sign-In prompt](/assets/blog/volunteer/7.png)

### Step 4: Complete Your Application

Share relevant skills, experience, preferred division, and availability. Include community work, projects, or event experience when relevant.

![Volunteer application form](/assets/blog/volunteer/8.png)

**Application tips:**

- Choose a division matching your strengths.
- State practical skills and tools you can use.
- Include event, community, or project experience.
- Be clear about availability before, during, and after event day.
- Mention other divisions you can support.

### Step 5: Submit and Track Your Application

Review details before submitting. Organizers review applications for selected event and division.

![Volunteer application submission confirmation](/assets/blog/volunteer/5.png)

You can view or revise active submissions from **My Applications** in account menu.

![My Applications menu item](/assets/blog/common/my-applications.png)

![Application details](/assets/blog/common/applications-details.png)

## What Happens Next

Submitting an application does not guarantee a volunteer slot. Organizers review applications based on division needs, availability, and team balance. Selected volunteers receive onboarding and schedule details directly.
