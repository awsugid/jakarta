AWS User Group Jakarta welcomes practitioners, builders, and community members who want to share useful AWS lessons.

> **AWS Community Day speakers:** Submit through the same [Speakers page](/speakers). Choose the Community Day CFP when it is listed as open. The same application guide also applies to monthly meetups and workshops.

## Current Opportunities

- **AWS Community Day:** sessions for our annual community conference.
- **Monthly meetups and workshops:** community talks, deep dives, lightning talks, and hands-on sessions.

Each open CFP identifies its event and session format before you apply.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Visit the Speakers Page

Open [Apply to Speak](/speakers). Review open CFPs, then choose the Community Day CFP for Community Day or another CFP matching your proposed session.

![Speakers page overview and Call for Speakers details](/assets/blog/speaker/1.png)

### Step 2: Start Your Application

Click **Apply to Speak** in the page hero or select an open session format.

![Call for Speakers introductory dialog](/assets/blog/speaker/2.png)

### Step 3: Sign In with Google

Continue with Google Sign-In. This matches your application, prevents duplicates, and lets you revisit active submissions.

![Google Sign-In prompt](/assets/blog/speaker/6.png)

### Step 4: Complete Your Proposal

Describe your session, target audience, technical level, and relevant experience. Include practical examples, lessons learned, or a demo plan when useful.

![Speaker application form](/assets/blog/speaker/7.png)

**Proposal tips:**

- State concrete attendee outcomes.
- Use specific AWS services, architecture decisions, or real-world lessons.
- Match scope to selected session format.
- Avoid product pitches.
- Explain required audience knowledge.

### Step 5: Submit and Track Your Application

Review details before submitting. Organizers review applications for selected event and session format.

![Speaker proposal submission confirmation](/assets/blog/speaker/5.png)

You can view or revise active submissions from **My Applications** in account menu.

![My Applications menu item](/assets/blog/common/my-applications.png)

![Application details](/assets/blog/common/applications-details.png)

## What Happens Next

Submitting an application does not guarantee a speaking slot. Organizers review proposals against event themes, audience needs, and agenda balance. Selected speakers receive schedule and preparation details directly.
