A hands-on Capture-The-Flag workshop on AWS.

AI agents now attack at machine speed. If one got into your AWS account today, could your team recover and prove the data is clean?

Mission Earth is under attack. An AI-driven adversary has compromised the cloud application behind its planetary monitoring network. Dashboards are unreliable, and the next mission decision depends on data your team can no longer trust.

In this team-based CTF on AWS, you'll face a controlled agentic attack simulation, investigate the evidence, contain the damage, and use Clumio to restore only what was hit. Flags are earned by verified recovery, not by finishing a restore job.

**Your team plays every side:**
- **The Attacker.** Solve cloud security puzzles to unlock real attacker moves.
- **The Defender.** Contain the damage and decide what to cut off, fast.
- **The Recovery Expert.** Restore only what was hit, keep valid changes, and prove it is clean.

Follow every team's recovery live on the big screen across three rounds, ending in an AI-speed boss round.

You’ll leave knowing how to scope an attack with evidence, recover only what was hit, and verify a recovery before you trust it, plus a checklist for your own account.

Built for cloud, platform, DevOps and security engineers. Work in teams with built-in hints, so all levels are welcome. **Please bring your laptop.**
