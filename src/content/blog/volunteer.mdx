We are plan to hosting monthly event regularly. Therefore, in order to make the event runs regularly, We need to make a talent pool for volunteers which can be filled by community members interested in contributing their skills.

### How to Join?

Our volunteer talent pool is always open. Follow these steps to add your profile and become part of our community of volunteers:

### Step 1: Visit the Volunteer Page

Navigate to our [Volunteer page](/volunteer) to explore the various volunteer divisions.

**What you'll see:**
- Modern, grid-based cards displaying open volunteer divisions.
- Roles, responsibilities, and technical/non-technical skills needed for each division.
- An **"Apply to Volunteer"** button to initiate the unified application process.

![Volunteer page overview and open divisions](/assets/blog/volunteer/1.png)

### Step 2: Open the Application Dialog

Click the **"Apply to Volunteer"** button in the hero section or on any division card. This launches the division selection menu right inside the page.

**What you'll see:**
- An interactive dialog prompting you to choose your preferred volunteer division.
- Compact division descriptions with custom icons for quick selection.

![Interactive volunteer division selection modal](/assets/blog/volunteer/2.png)

### Step 3: Securely Sign In with Google

Once you select your division and continue, you will be prompted to authenticate using Google Sign-In.

**Why we authenticate:**
- Secures your application profile.
- Syncs your volunteer application state.
- Automatically prevents duplicate applications and tracks status.

![Google Sign-In prompt with Developer Bypass](/assets/blog/volunteer/7.png)

### Step 4: Filling up the Form we Given

![Embedded FormBricks volunteer application form](/assets/blog/volunteer/8.png)

**Pro Tips for a Strong Application:**
- ✅ Be specific about your skills and experience.
- ✅ Mention any relevant projects or community work.
- ✅ Indicate your availability and time commitment.
- ✅ Show your passion for community building.
- ✅ Highlight both technical and soft skills.

### Step 5: Submit and Confirm

![Volunteer application success and submission confirmation](/assets/blog/volunteer/5.png)

**What happens next:**
- Your application is saved securely in our volunteer talent pool.
- Organizers will review your profile ahead of upcoming events.
- We will reach out 2-4 weeks before an event if we have openings in your division to confirm your availability!

### Editing the Submission

![Editing the submission](/assets/blog/common/my-applications.png)

You can view or revise your submission at any time by clicking you avatar in the top right corner and clicking on the **My Applications**

![](/assets/blog/common/applications-details.png)
