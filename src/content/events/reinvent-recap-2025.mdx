## Frequently Asked Questions
- **What is Commitment Fee?**
  - Commitment Fee is a fee that you give us to reserve your spot at the event. If you attend the event then follow the event until the end, you will receive a full refund of your commitment fee.
- **If i want to cancel my registration, what happens to my commitment fee?**
  - If you cancel your registration D-7 of the event, your commitment fee will be refunded. You can cancel your registration by clicking the "Cancel" button on the link provided in your confirmation email.
- **What happens if i miss the event?**
  - If you miss the event, your commitment fee will not be refunded. We only offer a full refund if you attend and stay in the evenet until the end. Other than that, we will not offer any refund.
- **What happens if i didn't attend the event until the end?**
  - If you didn't attend the event until the end, your commitment fee will not be refunded. We only offer a full refund if you attend and stay in the evenet until the end. Other than that, we will not offer any refund.
- **Can i transfer my commitment fee to another person?**
  - No, you cannot transfer your commitment fee to another person. The commitment fee is tied to your registration and cannot be transferred.
