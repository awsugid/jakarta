## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this event free?**
- Yes! This event is **completely free to attend**. We require a small commitment fee to reserve your spot, which is **fully refundable** when you attend and stay until the end of the event.

**What is the Commitment Fee?**
- The commitment fee is a **refundable deposit** to reserve your spot. If you **attend the full event, you receive a 100% refund**. This ensures we have accurate attendance numbers for planning.

**How do I cancel my registration?**
- You can cancel your registration **up to 7 days before the event** by clicking the "Cancel" button in your confirmation email. Your **commitment fee will be refunded**.

**What's the refund policy?**
- Your commitment fee is **refunded only if you attend and stay until the end** of the event. **Cancellations 7+ days before the event are also refunded**. If you miss the event or leave early, the fee is not refunded.

**Can I transfer my registration to someone else?**
- No, registrations are personal and cannot be transferred to another person.
