We are planing to hosting monthly event regularly. Therefore, in order to make the event runs regularly, We need to make a talent pool for speakers which can be filled by community members interested in sharing their knowledge.

### How to Join?

Our speaker talent pool is always open. Follow these steps to add your profile and become part of our community of speakers:

### Step 1: Visit the Speakers Page

Navigate to our [Speakers page](/speakers) to explore our Call for Speakers (CFP) program.

![Speakers page overview and Call for Speakers CFP details](/assets/blog/speaker/1.png)

### Step 2: Open the Call for Speakers Dialog

Click the **"Apply as a Speaker"** button in the hero section to initiate your CFP submission.

![Call for Speakers introductory confirmation modal](/assets/blog/speaker/2.png)

### Step 3: Securely Sign In with Google

Once you click continue, you will be prompted to authenticate using Google Sign-In.

**Why we authenticate the users:**
- Secures your speaker application profile.
- Syncs your session proposals and details.
- Lets you submit multiple talk proposals and edit your active submissions.

![Google Sign-In prompt with Developer Bypass](/assets/blog/speaker/6.png)

### Step 4: Filling up the Form we Given

![Embedded FormBricks Call for Speakers CFP form](/assets/blog/speaker/7.png)

**Pro Tips for a Strong Proposal:**
- ✅ Be specific about what attendees will learn.
- ✅ Include real-world examples or case studies.
- ✅ Mention if you'll have live demos or code samples.
- ✅ Be clear about the technical level required.
- ✅ Show your expertise and passion for the topic.

### Step 5: Submit and Confirm

![Speaker proposal success and submission confirmation](/assets/blog/speaker/5.png)

**What happens next:**
- Your profile and talk proposal are saved securely in our speaker talent pool.
- Organizers will review your proposal ahead of upcoming meetups.
- We will reach out 2-4 weeks before an event if your talk matches our schedule to confirm your availability!

### Editing the Submission 

![Editing the submission](/assets/blog/common/my-applications.png)

You can view or revise your submission at any time by clicking you avatar in the top right corner and clicking on the **My Applications**

![](/assets/blog/common/applications-details.png)
